How this works

  1. Share your link from the Send Link tab, or give someone your Partner ID directly — they type it into the "Enquire about this trip" form on any trip page.
  2. Admin reviews the enquiry and moves it through On-Hold → Resolved once the customer pays and an invoice is generated.
  3. Once a resolved booking is within 7 days of travel, the pax and commission are credited to you automatically — see the Sales tab.
  4. Pending payouts are paid out at the end of every 3rd month.
