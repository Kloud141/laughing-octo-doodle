Privacy Policy

  • Home
  • Privacy Policy
Last updated: 1 September 2026

This policy explains what information Embark Pedia collects when you browse this site, enquire about a roam, or book with us, and how that information is used.

Information we collect

We collect information in three ways: what you give us directly, what our systems record automatically, and what our service providers pass back to us.

  • Information you give us — your name, email, phone number and travel preferences when you fill in the contact form, create an account, or enquire about a roam.
  • Booking and payment details — passenger names, travel dates and payment confirmation. Card numbers are entered directly with our payment processor and never stored on our own servers.
  • Usage information — pages you visit, the device and browser you use, and how you arrived at the site, collected through standard server logs and cookies.
  • Communications — messages you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp, kept so we can follow up on an enquiry or booking.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and process bookings for roams and destinations.
  • To send booking confirmations, itinerary updates and other service messages.
  • To send newsletter updates about new roams and offers, only if you've subscribed, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve the site — understanding which destinations and roams visitors are most interested in.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Cookies & similar technologies

This site uses a small number of cookies to keep the site working as expected — remembering a search filter you've applied, or keeping you signed in. The Contact Us page also embeds a Google Maps view, which may set its own cookies once loaded; that embed is covered by Google's own privacy policy, not ours.

Sharing your information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only where it's needed to run the business:

  • With payment processors, solely to complete a transaction.
  • With accommodation, transport and local-experience partners involved in delivering a roam you've booked.
  • Where required by law, such as in response to a valid request from a tax or regulatory authority.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond to them, and booking records for as long as required for accounting and legal purposes. If you ask us to delete your details and no legal obligation requires us to keep them, we will.

Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • Send you a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct information that's out of date or inaccurate.
  • Delete your information, subject to any records we're legally required to retain.
  • Stop sending you marketing emails — every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link, or you can email us directly.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults able to enter into a travel booking contract. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how we handle personal information, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be flagged on the homepage.
