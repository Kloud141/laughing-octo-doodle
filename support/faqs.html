Answers to the questions we hear most often, grouped by topic. Can't find yours? The team is one message away.

Booking a roam

How do I book a roam?

Browse Featured Roams or the Destinations page, then reach out through the Contact Us form, phone or WhatsApp with the roam and dates you're interested in. We'll confirm availability and talk you through the next steps.

Can I customise an itinerary?

Yes. Every roam listed is a starting template rather than a fixed package — let us know what you'd like more or less of, and we'll adjust pacing, accommodation or add-on experiences where possible.

What's a comfortable group size?

Most roams run as small groups to keep things personal, and we also take solo travelers, couples and private groups — just mention your group size when you enquire.

Payments & pricing

How much deposit do I need to pay?

Deposit amounts vary by roam and are confirmed when we send your itinerary and quote. The balance is due ahead of departure, as set out in our Terms of Service.

What payment methods do you accept?

Payments are taken securely through our payment processor using major debit/credit cards and standard bank transfer. We don't accept payment details over email or WhatsApp.

Documents & preparation

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your nationality and the destination. Visa and permit requirements are your responsibility, as set out in our Terms of Service, but we're glad to point you to the right resources for your route.

Should I take travel insurance?

Yes — we strongly recommend arranging comprehensive travel insurance as soon as your booking is confirmed, covering trip cancellation, medical emergencies and baggage.

Cancellations

What if I need to cancel?

Refunds depend on how far ahead of departure you cancel. The full breakdown is on our Cancellation Policy page.

What happens if Embark Pedia cancels a roam?

If we need to cancel a confirmed roam for reasons within our control, you'll receive a full refund or the option to transfer to another roam. See our Terms of Service for how we handle cancellations outside anyone's control.

Didn't find your answer?

Send us a message and we'll get back to you personally.
